---

**Wiki** documentation is **intention**-based, and often outdated.
**Living** documentation is **up-to-date**, **automated**.

To close the gap between intended and deployed.

---

Developers/AI needs **digital contracts** and **guidelines**, to do the right thing and the thing right.

- functional, non-functional
- business model, business rules, business processes
- API and UX specifications, and catalogues
- architecture and code design patterns, UX design system

---

Usual up-front **practices** like Process modelling, Domain modelling, Story mapping, and Example mapping should deliver **the right digital artefacts**.

---

Analysts, Designers, Maintainers, Support, Ops, needs **living and observable facts** about the real state.

---

Documentation should be organized as

- **Doc at rest**: wiki
- **Doc as code**: git, to leverage git-ops, to be close to development chain
- **Living doc**: hubs feeded by automation pipelines

---

**Effective** software development is **connected**.
Every stage should produce the expected digital value for the next one.
